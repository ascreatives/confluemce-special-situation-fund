01 / Overview

A Focused Pathway Into Private Markets.

The Fund is an Australian-domiciled private investment vehicle that gives wholesale investors access to private equity and pre-IPO opportunities in the United States, targeting growth-stage and later-stage companies with operating histories, recurring revenue and scalable business models.

    05 / Process

    Process, Horizon & Exit

    A disciplined investment approach combining proprietary deal flow, structured due diligence and staged capital deployment. Investments are assessed for strategic fit, downside protection and a defined exit pathway.

    1–3Years Target Holding Period
    $500K–$1MTypical Investment Tickets

    Exits may be pursued through trade sales, strategic acquisitions or other appropriate realisation routes. Timing and proceeds depend on market conditions and the circumstances of each investment and are not guaranteed.

    Liquidity Notice

    Private investments are illiquid. Investors should expect capital to remain committed throughout the holding period and potentially longer. Distributions depend on successful realisations, and no exit timing or return is assured.
